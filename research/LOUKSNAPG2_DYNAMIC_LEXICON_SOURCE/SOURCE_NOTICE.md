# Perseus Dynamic Lexicon source data

The upstream Greek and Latin Dynamic Lexicon archives are retained for controlled,
reproducible linguistic-resource construction. They are research inputs only and are
not part of the Louksnapg2 runtime package or its 490,000,000-byte distribution budget.

The upstream project describes these as automatically generated Greek and Latin
lexical entries and aligned parallel-text data with automatic morphological and
syntactic analysis. Automatically induced entries can contain noise. Preserve
attribution, license, original hashes, ambiguity, sense, morphology and corpus
provenance. These files alone do not create or certify a Greek-Latin transducer;
the initial importer is inventory-only.
