# HARD PROHIBITIONS

1. No canonical mutation, authority transfer, identity reassignment or certification propagation.
2. No silent rename, delete, merge, substitute, overwrite, duplicate, reauthorize, reclassify or downgrade.
3. No `UNKNOWN -> PASS`.
4. No `WORKFLOW_SUCCESS == MISSION_SUCCESS`.
5. No self-certification by CUSTOSZ, Runtime, MetaOS, producer workflow or candidate component.
6. No new capability identity before exact duplicate search against Louksna, Runtime and the CUSTOSZ V7 capability registry.
7. No full-corpus materialization in Git for massive corpora.
8. No large model weights or generated book bodies in ordinary Git history.
9. No heavy local user-host workload unless explicitly authorized for a task that cannot be cloud-executed.
10. No long-lived secrets in repository files or artifacts.
11. No market/stream claim without source identity, timestamp, freshness/staleness and provenance.
12. No theological conclusion presented as empirical market evidence; no empirical market observation presented as doctrinal authority.
13. No Austrian, mainstream or other economic lens silently promoted to raw fact.
14. No translation PASS without segment identity, source linkage, terminology controls and independent QA evidence.
15. No training before capability/evidence admission and no trained candidate bypassing the complete assurance pipeline.
16. No G24 before a genuine independent G23 on the same frozen candidate digest.
17. No external plugin, account or provider connection performed automatically merely because it is useful.
18. No destructive pruning by the lifecycle hygiene layer without explicit authorization, dependency proof and rollback.
